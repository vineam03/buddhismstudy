Guided Study

Nine units · logical sequence · progress tracking

The whole site as a course: from the shape of the problem through mechanics, ethics, mind-training, and relational practice — each unit with ordered readings, a rationale, and a practice assignment. Checkboxes remember your progress.

The Curriculum

Four stages · dukkha → mechanics → path → relational living

A structured study path from the universal predicament through psychological mechanics and daily implementation to the four Brahmavihāras — each stage anchored to specific canonical texts, with notes on what English translations lose.

The Canon, Cover to Cover

All three baskets · every book

Every division of the Tipiṭaka — the full Vinaya, all five Nikāyas including each book of the Khuddaka, and all seven Abhidhamma books — explained in contemporary English and mapped to the curriculum stages.

The Sutta Library

67 key texts · study notes

"Spark notes" for the canon's essential suttas — the gist of each text, what it teaches about truth and human nature, and how to apply it in daily life. Searchable by theme: anger, money, grief, speech, breath.

The Pātimokkha

Vinaya Piṭaka · Suttavibhaṅga

All 227 rules of the bhikkhu Pātimokkha, organized by category, each with the origin story that prompted the Buddha to lay it down.

Modern Life

Career · love · money · conflict · identity

The canon aimed directly at contemporary life: promotion treadmills, relationship repair, debt and burnout, comment-section anger, imposter syndrome — and the short list of universal truths that survived every century.

Journey to the West

Xiyouji · the mind-monkey's pilgrimage

The great Chinese allegory read episode by episode: the golden headband as discipline, Pigsy as appetite, the counterfeit self, the bottomless boat, the blank scriptures — each mapped to the principles taught on this site.
