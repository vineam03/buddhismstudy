Each rule in the Vinaya comes with an origin story (from the Suttavibhaṅga): a monk did something, complaints followed, and the Buddha laid down a rule. The summaries below condense both the rule and its story. Click any rule to expand it; each links to the full canonical text at SuttaCentral. Many rules were prompted by the same recurring characters — especially the misbehaving "group of six" monks (chabbaggiyā), the greedy Upananda, and the lust-prone Udāyin.
